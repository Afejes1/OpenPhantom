# Affine matrix helper acceptance

Six campaign044 exact helpers share the asserted48-byte matrix and12-byte vector layouts. Five wrappers use a natural48-byte temporary, pass the complete builder result to the correct composer and preserve observed argument order. The sixth directly adds three vector components to translation in sequence. Three addresses are team-not_done and three team-reported_done; these priorities remain separate from local verification.

The shared fixture runs all five wrappers through distinct builder/composer dispatch pairs and guarded matrix/vector pools. It checks disjoint or exact-translation input alias, complete temporary payload, callback order and callback-visible mutations. Builders and composers remain authored observation backends; their matrix mathematics are not reconstructed by these wrappers.

Direct translation retains the independently reviewed literal binary32 expectations for signed zero, cancellation, rounding ties, subnormals, overflow, infinities and exact input alias. Floating-point control is saved/restored around the bounded cases. Arbitrary partial overlap and other control-word settings are outside this fixture domain.

The two campaign045 palette builders remain research and are not integrated or accepted. No previous target is changed, and no byte exclusion or warning waiver is added. Whole-executable identity remains pending.
